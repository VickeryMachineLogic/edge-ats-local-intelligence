# Edge ATS — Local Recruiting Intelligence

**A private recruiting hub — skill gaps, market intelligence, and dual agents — running on local models so candidate and employer data stays in-house.**

Edge ATS is built for recruiting desks that need more than a resume pile. Analysis runs locally (Ollama), so confidential candidate and client information never has to leave your office — and the system is owned by you, not rented from a SaaS platform.

## What it does for you

- **Analytics Hub** — your whole book at a glance: clients, prospects, roles, talent pool, and a knowledge graph
- **Skills Matrix** — gap analysis showing where demand outruns supply
- **Market Intelligence** — skills-gap charts to guide outreach and placement
- **Dual agents** — Employer Advocate and Candidate Advocate argue both sides, with confidential fields withheld as needed
- **Magic Batch Ingest** — drop in resumes, companies, and roles; a background Graph RAG queue does the rest
- **Local LLM** — private by default, sovereign, yours

## Who it's for

Recruiting offices and staffing firms that want AI-assisted matching and market insight without sending sensitive data to someone else's cloud.
